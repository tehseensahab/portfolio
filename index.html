What I do

Klaviyo, run properly

Flows and campaigns built around how the product actually gets used — not a generic welcome-and-abandon-cart template pack.

Replenishment-cycle logic

Timing reorder prompts to when a jar, bag, or bottle is actually running low — the difference between a discount blast and a message that lands.

Education-first onboarding

New customers get taught how and why to use the product before they're asked to buy again. Fewer refunds, more second orders.

Subscription LTV

Working the Recharge or Skio layer alongside email — save flows, cadence options, and win-back logic that keep subscribers subscribed.

The replenishment cycle

Consumable products run on a cycle, not a funnel. Every message I write sits on a specific point of it.
