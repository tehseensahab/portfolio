If you sell something people reorder, this is for you

Most retention problems in ecommerce aren't a Klaviyo problem — they're a timing and data problem. Here's where that usually shows up.

Problem

Flows built on a generic clock

A 30-day post-purchase sequence doesn't know if your product runs out in 3 weeks or 3 months. I build reorder timing around actual usage, not a template default.

Problem

Rising ad costs, flat repeat rate

If new-customer acquisition is doing all the work, margins shrink every quarter. I build the retention and subscription layer that takes pressure off ad spend.

Problem

No real visibility into what's working

I run structured A/B tests and segment by behavior, not just purchase date, so decisions come from data — not a hunch about what the next email should say.

Problem

Subscribers who quietly churn

Save flows, flexible cadence options, and win-back sequences timed to when someone's actually at risk of cancelling — not after they already have.
